## [Section: The Three Names]
### Why "James * Jimmy * Jido"?

You will see three names featured at the top of this website. Each represents a different era and facet of my life:

---

### **JAMES: The Official Persona**
*   **The Origin:** This name was rarely used in my personal life unless absolutely necessary. In fact, prior to adulthood, I hadn't heard it used since my mother was angry with me around 1962!
*   **The Transition:** I restarted using "James" when I began traveling extensively, as my passport required my official name. From there, it became necessary to use it on all official correspondence and business phone calls.
*   **What it Represents:** In my everyday routine, **James** represents my official side—the financial guy, the tax representative, the bank person, and anything that demands government-issued identification.

---

### **JIMMY: The Creative Dreamer**
*   **The Origin:** Originally, this was a childhood nickname used primarily by my sisters, which I had long forgotten. Many years later, a work colleague began using it regularly as a term of endearment, and it stuck. It spread quickly through my workplace and eventually among my friends outside of work.
*   **What it Represents:** In my everyday life, **Jimmy** is the social, creative, and musical side of me. He is the guy playing guitar and truly living the dream!

---

### **JIDO: The Family Legacy**
*   **The Origin:** This term is relatively new to me, and I treat it with reverence and respect. My step-grandchildren are part Arabic, and "Jido" (often written as *Jeddou* or *Jiddo*) is the Arabic word for a grandfather. This is what the grandkids call me.
*   **What it Represents:** Because this website is designed in large part as a digital repository for their future interest, **Jido** represents the grandfather looking out for their future, well-being, and family history.
