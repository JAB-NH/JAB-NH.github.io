## Who Am I?

To different people and in different parts of my life, I have been known by three distinct names: **James**, **Jimmy**, and **Jido**. Each represents a different side of my journey:

*   **James**: The official side. The name on my passport, financial documents, tax forms, and professional business correspondence.
*   **Jimmy**: The social and creative side. A term of endearment from work colleagues and friends. Jimmy is the guy who is out there living the dream, playing guitar, and enjoying life.
*   **Jido**: The family side. This is the Arabic name for a grandfather, which is how my step-grandchildren address me. It represents my role as a guardian, looking out for their future and keeping these records for them.

*You can read the full story behind these names and my partner Norma on the **[About Us](file:///c:/Users/j-a-b/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/google_site/proposals/v1_modernized/about.md)** page.*
