Tunes: My Album

For many years, friends and family have heard me mention an "album." Well, for better or worse, this is it—mistakes and all. It is a little late now to correct the past, so there is no need to listen too carefully!

This is an eclectic collection spanning over 35 years of playing. It contains primarily original tunes and songs, recovered from very old recording room test tapes. Almost no one has ever heard me play guitar because I rarely played outside my own home.

Note on my journey: A slowly progressing Benign Tremor in both of my hands has finally curtailed my playing for good. But hey, "Better Late Than Never," right?

The Track List

1. Look Into My Eyes (3:38)

The Story: My first-ever love song. I was 21, working my first real job, and fell head-over-heels for my boss's wife at dinner. The result was this soul-searching look into the heart of a young boy.

Technical: Recorded circa 1971 on a brand new AKAI reel-to-reel tape deck. You can hear volume fluctuations during the first 10 seconds.

Download Audio

2. Dual in AM (1:42)

The Story: An early overdubbing attempt. The deck allowed me to play a rhythm track and then replay it twice to overlay the "lead" guitar. It created an increasing echo with each track!

Download Audio

3. Dual in Em (2:41)

The Story: A softer overdubbing output and my first experiment in Open E tuning. I learned this open-tuning technique after watching Joni Mitchell play open string a mere 10 feet in front of me at an Ottawa coffee house for $2.

4. One Day (4:07)

The Story: My cover of Murray McLauchlan's "Revelations." It became my true personal anthem. I didn't realize I had been playing it slightly wrong for a while and missed recording the first chorus!
