Haddad Family Photographs
Step-by-Step Guide to Downloading Photos & Running the Family Slideshow
1. GO TO:
Website: norgem.ca → Personal (James-Jimmy-Jido)
2. PICK:
Who Am I → Family Pictures → Haddad Family
Stages to Photo Downloading
Stage 1 — SELECT Decade and/or Photos (or Select All Photos)
  • The system will automatically let you know the size of your selection.
  • Switches are toggles — at least one switch MUST be selected.
  • 1940s is selected as the default — click a switch twice to turn it off.
  • Selecting a decade toggle queues ALL photos from that decade into your basket.
Stage 2 — FILL Your Basket with Selected Photos and/or Decades
  • You can select multiple decades and/or individual photos.
  • The bracketed number (?) shows the quantity of photos chosen.
  • The bottom of the page shows (? MB) for the total download file size.
  • Select ‘Fill Basket’ to choose ALL photos in the entire collection.
Stage 3 — DOWNLOAD Your Filled Basket at Page Bottom
  • Be sure to include the ‘Slideshow (HTML + JS)’ option.
  • Choose ‘Empty Basket’ at any time if you wish to start over.
  • Choose ‘Immediate Download (.ZIP)’ to retrieve your photos.
Stage 4 — COMPLETED Download & Viewing Your Slideshow
  • File Manager will open when your download is ready.
  • It is recommended to save/extract this file to a known location (for example, your Desktop).
  • The system will automatically create a new ‘Haddad Family’ folder there, along with the entire photo decade folder structure.
  • Open the ‘Haddad Family’ folder and double-click Norgem_Family_Slideshow_Player.html.
  • The system will automatically locate and load all of your downloaded images.
  • Customize and run the Slideshow at your convenience (you may wish to create a Desktop shortcut to the Slideshow player).
⚠ IMPORTANT — DO NOT:
Delete, Move, or Rename ANY Folder or File Under and Including ‘Haddad Family’
